---
title: 'Concatenating strings'
question: 'Does memoizing `fullName` help?'
---

## Expected output

`John Doe`

## Why

Joining two strings is trivial, and strings are **primitives**, so a freshly computed `"John Doe"` is already equal (`Object.is`) to the previous one. Any child or effect that receives `fullName` sees no change either way.

The memo doesn't help with speed or with stability.

## Takeaway

Just compute it inline:

```tsx
const fullName = `${firstName} ${lastName}`;
```
